About

The name says it: your numbers,
at their highest point.

Apogee is the peak of an orbit, the farthest, highest reach. We want to see your numbers at their highest point, that is, accurate, clear, and working at their best for you.

Meet the founder

Doris Velázquez, MBA

Numbers have always been my passion. I founded Apogee Numbers on a simple belief: business owners make better decisions when their numbers are clean, current, and clearly explained. My mission is to help entrepreneurs reach their financial apogee.

With a B.S. in Accounting and an MBA, I pair the discipline of accounting fundamentals with a broader view of how the numbers drive your business. The accounting expertise keeps your books precise, compliant, and dependable. The MBA lets me step back and see the whole picture, so I don't just record what happened, I help you understand what it means and what to do next. If you ever want managerial guidance on pricing, profitability, or growth, it's already built into the person keeping your books.

As a QuickBooks Online Certified ProAdvisor, I help small businesses set up their books the right way, catch up and clean up messy records, and stay organized month after month.

Based in Collegedale, Tennessee, I work with clients remotely across the country. My aim is to give you back your time and hand you numbers you can trust. That's what Apogee Numbers is here for: strategic financial support for growing businesses.

Doris Velázquez, MBA · Founder
