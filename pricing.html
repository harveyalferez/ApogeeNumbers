Good to know

Pricing questions

How is my monthly price determined?

It's based on your transaction volume, the number of accounts to reconcile, and overall complexity, not billed by the hour. After your discovery call and a quick look at your books, you'll get a flat monthly quote that stays predictable.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-in. Monthly bookkeeping is month-to-month. I simply ask for reasonable notice so we can hand off your file cleanly if your needs change.

What if I'm behind and also need ongoing help?

Common situation. We'll handle the catch-up as a one-time cleanup project to get your file current, then move you onto a monthly plan to keep it that way.

Do you handle my taxes?

Apogee Numbers focuses on bookkeeping and advisory. I keep your books accurate and tax-ready, and I'm glad to coordinate directly with your CPA or tax preparer at filing time.
